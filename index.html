Case Study 01 · Beauty

Creating a more cohesive digital presence for a beauty brand.

The Challenge

The salon had an inconsistent social media presence, unclear branding, and low audience engagement.

The Strategy

We developed a content strategy focused on showcasing services, building trust, and creating content relevant to its ideal customers.

The Execution

We created branded visuals, planned a strategic content calendar, and optimized the profile to create a more professional online presence.

The Outcome

The brand achieved a more cohesive digital presence designed to improve visibility, engagement, and customer interest.

Case Study 02 · Fitness

Building a stronger, more engaging fitness presence.

The fitness business struggled with inconsistent posting and lacked a clear strategy to engage and grow its audience.

The Strategy

We created a social media strategy focused on motivational, educational, and community-driven content.

The Execution

We developed branded content, structured a consistent posting schedule, and used performance insights to guide content decisions.

The Outcome

The brand established a stronger and more engaging online presence designed to support audience growth and brand awareness.
